Safety & privacy features

Pre-join safety check

When enabled, you must confirm you reviewed the game details and public meeting place before sending a join request.

Location privacy reminder

When enabled, the Create Game form reminds hosts that the venue is visible to players. Use a public place, never a home address.

Friend requests

Friend requests are private to the two accounts involved and stay pending until accepted. You can decline or remove a friendship.

Game invitations

Hosts can invite accepted friends to a game. Invited friends must accept before they join and take an open spot.

Game chat and respectful language

Only the host and joined players can read or send game chat messages. Common profanity is blocked when messages are sent.

Browser-only preferences

Safety switches are saved on this device and browser. They do not sync across devices or enforce server-side account privacy yet.

Reporting

Reports are not submitted to RALLY yet. If there is immediate danger, move to safety and contact a trusted adult or local emergency services.

Before you meet

  • Choose a public or supervised venue.
  • Review the game time, venue, organizer, and age-group information.
  • Keep your home address, phone number, and private contact details off public game listings.
  • Tell someone you trust where you're going and when you expect to return.
